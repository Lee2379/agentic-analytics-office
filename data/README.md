# Demo data

Both CSV files are synthetic and were created for this repository. They contain no customer, employee, workspace, account, or scraped product identifiers.

- `sample_products.csv`: a fictional assortment used for descriptive market metrics.
- `sample_sales.csv`: 35 chronological daily observations with a trend and weekly variation.

The values are deliberately small enough to inspect by hand. They are not intended to represent a real company or market.
